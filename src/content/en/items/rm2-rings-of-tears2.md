This is a second Ring of Tears, a photomontage--a megatomb--memorializing the 174,000,000 murdered by governments 1900-1999. It merges photos of these people being murdered with their resulting corpses, bones, and skulls. For documentation on the number murdered and the what, when, where, and who, see this web site. For the photos that were used in the megatomb, go to Photographs of Democide: [Room 2](RM2.PHOTOS.ROOM2.HTM).

The final megatomb, [Rings of Tears: Faces of Death](RM3.RINGS.OF.TEARS3.HTM), displays the faces of those about to die, most knowing it. The photos for this megatomb are at Photographs of Democide: [Room 3](RM3.PHOTOS.ROOM3.HTM).

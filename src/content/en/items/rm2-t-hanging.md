Among the first murdered by the Young Turks in their planned genocide of Turkey's Armenian minority in 1915 were the Armenian intellectuals. Here is shown a hanging of several of them. Documentation: see [_Death By Government_](NOTE1.HTM) and [_Statistics of Democide_](NOTE5.HTM). Source: ["The Armenian Genocide"](http://imia.cc.duth.gr/turkey/arme.e.html).**

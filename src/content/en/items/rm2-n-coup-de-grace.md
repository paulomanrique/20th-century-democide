**The Holocaust. Here is a photograph of a German policeman finishing off Jewish women who remain alive in a ravine south of Rovno in Ukraine. These women had been taken from the Mizocz ghetto on its liquidation, forced to strip, and shot en mass. Documentation: see [_Democide_](NOTE3.HTM). Source: This is #17878 in the collection of Holocaust photographs displayed by the [United States Memorial Museum](http://www.ushmm.org/uia-bin/uia_form/photos).**

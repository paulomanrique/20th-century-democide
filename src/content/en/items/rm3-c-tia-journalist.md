**In June 1989 Chinese troops and tanks crushed prodemocracy demonstrations, the largest massacre occurring in Tiananmen Square, Beijing, in which perhaps 2,600 students and supporters were murdered. Here is shown a photo of one of the victims, a journalist who would be shot a few days after this picture was taken For documentation on communist China's democide and repression, see [_China's Bloody Century_](NOTE2.HTM). Source: ["Tiananmen, April-June 1989"](http://www.christusrex.org/www1/sdc/massacre.html).**

This is a third Ring of Tears, a photomontage--a megatomb--memorializing the 174,000,000 murdered by governments 1900-1999. It merges faces of those about to be murdered with photos of those being murdered and the corpses, bones, and skulls of those that went before. For documentation on the number murdered and the what, when, where, and who, see this web site. For the photos that were used in the megatomb, go to Photographs of Democide: [Room 3](RM3.PHOTOS.ROOM3.HTM).

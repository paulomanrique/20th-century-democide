**Germans are about to murder this old lady in the Belzec concentration camp. Documentation: [_Democide: Nazi Genocide and Mass Murder_](NOTE3.HTM). Source: ["The Undeniable Holocaust."](http://209.207.141.249/gallery/galleryitems/holocaust/holocaust11.htm) From Prof. Leopold Pfefferberg-Page Collection, via the Main Commission for the Investigation of Nazi War Crimes, Warsaw, Poland.  
  
I was so depressed by this photo-night after night I kept seeing it--that I finally tried to make [a painting](RM3.N.OLD.WOMEN2.HTM) of how I saw it--this poor old lady shivering in the snow alone, no friends or love ones around to give her support and courage, waiting to be murdered.**

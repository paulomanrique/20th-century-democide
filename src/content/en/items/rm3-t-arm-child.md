**This is a photograph of a starving Armenian child in rags, a victim of Turkey's genocide. The child likely died soon after this photo was taken. Documentation: [_Death By Government_](NOTE1.HTM), and [_Statistics of Democide_](NOTE5.HTM). Source: [" Pictures of the Armenian Genocide."](http://www.cilicia.com/armo10f.html) This and other pictures of the genocide are from James Nazer's book, _The First Genocide Of The 20th Century: The Story Of The Armenian Massacres In Text And Pictures_, and _The Armenian Genocide As Reported In The Press_, by Richard Kloian.

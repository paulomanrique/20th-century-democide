**There were many Tiananmen Square like demonstrations in a number of Chinese cities, and all were equally squashed with force. The Chinese National TV reported on June 16, 1989, for example, that a Shanghai Court had sentenced three men to death for their involvement in the "anti-government riot" of June 6. Here is a photo of one of the three. For documentation on communist China's democide and repression, see [_China's Bloody Century_](NOTE2.HTM); and [Chapter 1](WF.CHAP1.HTM) of [_Saving Lives, Enriching Life_](NOTE15.HTM) Source: ["Index Of The Tam Photo Series: Massacre"](http://www.cnd.org:8022/June4th/massacre.html).**

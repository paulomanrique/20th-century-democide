**  
Although the Nazis murdered around 15,000,000 non-Jews to 5,200,000 Jews, there are few photos on the internet that I could find other than those identified as picturing the Holocaust. Here is possibly one. No matter one or a hundred, let each one of these photograph, whether of the Holocaust or not, be symbolic of the near 21,000,000 the Nazis murdered. Here the photo shows German soldiers murdering by gunfire a group of Soviet civilians. They are kneeling by the side of a mass grave already holding many killed. These civilians may be some of the Soviet communists, former high government officials, and professionals that the Nazis systematically hunted down and murdered. Documentation: see [_Democide_](NOTE3.HTM). Source: ["The Undeniable Holocaust"](http://209.207.141.249/gallery/galleryitems/holocaust/holocaust09.htm). From the National Archives, College Park, Maryland.**
